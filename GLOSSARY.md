# SportsWeb

A sports schedule aggregation and digest application that provides personalized weekly schedules, marquee game discovery, and calendar synchronization.

## Language

**Team**:
A professional franchise or collegiate athletic program competing in a recognized sports league.
_Avoid_: Club, organization

**Fixture**:
A scheduled, live, or completed sports match between two teams at a specific date and time.
_Avoid_: Game, event, matchup

**Subscription**:
A user's intent to follow a team, automatically adding that team's fixtures to their schedule.
_Avoid_: Follow, favorite, watch

**Pinned Match**:
An individual fixture explicitly added to a user's schedule without requiring a subscription to either participating team.
_Avoid_: Pinned game, bookmarked match

**User Schedule**:
The consolidated, chronologically ordered collection of fixtures derived from a user's team subscriptions and pinned matches.
_Avoid_: Calendar, feed, agenda

**Excitement Index**:
A calculated 0–100 score rating fixture significance based on AP rankings, betting spreads, national broadcast slots, and historic rivalries.
_Avoid_: Match rating, importance score, buzz score

**Digest**:
A scheduled periodic summary of upcoming fixtures delivered to a user via email or dashboard view.
_Avoid_: Newsletter, bulletin, notification
